What is AI?

Sawyer Frey

AI is a groundbreaking technology where a machine such as a computer can think & problem solve similarly to humans with data. It has has been getting very popular recently but has been around for quite some time too. Many fields benefit from AI, such as:

Healthcare
Finance
Transportation
Education
Entertainment

Generally, AI models learn from analyzing MASSIVE amounts of data. Among the data, it uses algorithms to find patterns. It uses the data it analyzed to later make predictions or decisions. Then, it alters its algorithms based on what it learned.
To do this, AI is built up of high-tech computers with TONS of video cards. The video cards do most of the work for AI to function.

As a concept as broad as AI, there are different types which differ greatly. They have different purposes, goals, & functions. Here are a few examples:

Narrow AI
Narrow AI is a simplistic form of AI built to perform a single task efficently. They excel in this one task, & cannot perform functions outside of what its been programmed to do. An example of Narrow AI are voice assistants like Siri or Alexa.
General AI & Superintelligent AI
Both of these AI are hypothetical & haven't been made yet. General AI could understand & learn any task that a human can, & would have human-like intelligence.
Superintelligent AI (SAI) is a step above General AI. SAI would be able to outperform any human in every task, & would have the ability to learn on its own.
Reactive AI
This describes an AI that can react to immediate actions but cannot learn from past experiences or improve their functionality.
Self-Aware AI
A hypothetical, hyperintelligent AI that would have emotional capabilities & be able to sense the emotions of others.
Generative AI
A relatively new type of AI that can generate images, text, or sounds based on a given prompt.

AI is prevalent in everyday life, & I often use AI to assist with tasks. An example of an AI you may interact with daily are social media algorithms. They analyze user metrics & interests to give relevant posts/topics. Another example is generative AI such as ChatGPT or Copilot.
